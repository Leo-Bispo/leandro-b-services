Cancellation & Refund Policy

Scheduled services

You can cancel or reschedule a scheduled service free of charge with at least 24 hours' notice. To do so, email leandrobisp.s@gmail.com with your booking details. If you give less notice, please get in touch to discuss the options.

Issues with completed work

Please report any issue with a completed job within 48 hours. Leandro B Services will arrange to fix the issue or provide an appropriate partial refund. This reporting period does not limit your statutory rights if an issue becomes apparent later.

Website projects

Deposits cover work already carried out. If a project is cancelled, a pro rata refund is provided for work not started, based on the agreed quote and progress made.

Your statutory rights

This policy is in addition to your statutory consumer rights. Where a statutory 14-day cancellation period applies to a service booked remotely, you may cancel within 14 days of agreeing the contract. If you expressly ask for work to start during that period, you may have to pay a proportionate amount for work supplied before cancellation. The right to cancel ends after full performance only with your prior express consent and acknowledgement.

Any refund due under a statutory cancellation right will be made within 14 days of being told of the cancellation.
