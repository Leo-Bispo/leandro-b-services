Privacy Policy

Who handles your information

Leandro Bispo, trading as Leandro B Services in London (NW9), is responsible for your personal information. For privacy enquiries or requests, email leandrobisp.s@gmail.com.

What is collected and why

Only the contact and job details needed to prepare a quote, deliver the agreed services and issue invoices are collected. This may include your name, email address, job location and details you provide about the work.

Under UK GDPR, this information is used to take steps at your request before a contract and to fulfil the contract. Invoice records are also kept where needed to meet legal tax and accounting obligations.

Payments and service providers

Card payments are processed by Stripe. Leandro B Services does not receive or store your full card details. Relevant information may be handled by providers used for email, banking, payments and website hosting to supply those services. Your personal data is not sold.

This website does not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process technical connection information, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure the service.

Retention and your rights

Information is kept only as long as needed for the enquiry, service, payment and any applicable record-keeping obligations, then deleted when no longer needed.

Depending on the circumstances, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or transfer of your data, or object to its use. Email the address above to make a request. You can also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK data protection regulator.
